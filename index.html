About

I'm a second-year student in the Erasmus Mundus master's in Intelligent Field Robotic Systems (first year at the University of Girona, this semester at ELTE in Budapest). Before the master's I spent three years deploying AGVs and autonomous mobile robots in factories and warehouses with ROS, ROS 2 and Nav2.

Most of what I enjoy happens when a robot leaves simulation: clocks drift, sensors disagree, and maps get noisy around glass. I like finding out why and fixing it. This year I also co-authored a detection benchmark paper (spotlight, ISIC Workshop at MICCAI 2026) and built a graph neural network as a research intern at ViCOROB.

I'm looking for a master's thesis in industry from February 2027, in navigation, perception, SLAM or robot learning.
